Everything you need to know about ordering, shipping, and custom crew designs.

Shipping Information

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the continental US.

  • Orders over $75 ship free within the continental US.
  • Express shipping (1-2 days) available at checkout for +$15.
  • FPO/APO/DPO orders ship via USPS Priority Mail flat rate.
  • International shipping available to select countries. Calculated at checkout.
  • Crew orders (10+ sweaters) ship in one batch to a single address or individual addresses.

Shipping status is shown as "Underway" once your order leaves the warehouse. Track your order with your order reference number.

Sizing Guide

Our sweaters run true to size. If you plan to layer over a uniform, go one size up.

  • S: 34-36" chest
  • M: 38-40" chest
  • L: 40-42" chest
  • XL: 42-44" chest
  • XXL: 46-48" chest
  • XXXL: 50-52" chest

Use our Size Recommender tool above for a personalized recommendation based on height, weight, and build.

Custom Crew Orders

Custom crew orders are our specialty. Whether it's a ship, squadron, or command party, we make it happen.

  • Minimum order: 12 sweaters for custom crew designs.
  • Choose ship type, hull number, warfare community, phrase, and personalization.
  • Design proof provided within 3-5 business days.
  • Production time: 3-4 weeks after proof approval.
  • Size collection: we provide a spreadsheet template for collecting sizes.
  • Group discount: 15% off orders of 20+, 25% off orders of 50+.

Example: USS NIMITZ CVN-68 / DECK THE HULLS / WESTPAC CHRISTMAS 2026

Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love your ugly sweater. If it doesn't fit or isn't ugly enough, we'll help.

  • Returns accepted within 30 days of delivery.
  • Item must be unworn with tags attached.
  • Free return shipping on defective items.
  • Custom crew orders are non-returnable once produced.
  • Exchanges for different sizes are free (subject to availability).
